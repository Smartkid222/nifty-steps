Our History

Nifty Steps School started operation in October 2008 with the objective to provide a unique form of education to the Nigerian children. Our population has grown steadily from just five pupils and three teachers in the kindergarten class in 2008, to a current figure of 155 pupils and 21 teachers. Over 85% of these teachers are either NCE graduate or hold a degree in Education. Three others are currently in the middle of their NCE certification course.

Our Motto, crescat scienta vita excolatur, which is Latin for Ã¢â‚¬Å“let knowledge grow; let life increase,Ã¢â‚¬Â underpins our earnest desire to provide an education designed to enable our children to discover their strengths, and to make the most of their talents in life.

The held belief at Steps School is that when a child is carefully guided, he/she can begin, even at tender age, to tap on the inner resources that can lead to achieving his/her full life potentials. It is no wonder that our pioneer pupils who are now in Primary 1, are not only articulate in mathematics but are also today well able to read, even newspapers, as well as write in simple sentences. We have since 2008 established so good a testimonial that many parents have mounted pressure on us to enroll children in higher classes beyond the level of our pioneer pupils. We now run classes up to Primary 4.

April 21, 2008

School is registered as Steps Primary and Secondary School, with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC).

October 20, 2008

Formally commenced classes with with two teachers, four Kindergarten (KG 1) toddlers, and one Primary 1 pupil.

March 6, 2012

Registered the school as Nifty Steps Primary School with the Lagos State Ministry of Education.

March 17, 2015

Granted approval to present students for BECE (JSS3 exam).

October 15, 2015

Re-registered the school as Nifty Steps Primary School and College with the Corporate Affairs Commission.

August 20, 2018

Received formal letters of approval from the Lagos State Government, enabling Nifty Steps School to internally present students for WASC and NECO, as well as the primary Six Leaving Certificate.

Our Mission

To provide a unique and distinctive education aimed at moulding each pupil to be a self-confident, inquiring, positive and tolerant person with a well-rounded character and an independent mind which will enable the child stand up for himself/herself and be of value to the larger community.

Our Vision

To be the school of first choice anywhere we operate, positively shaping the consciousness of our students to consistently stand up in their communities as individuals who contribute immensely to humanity.
